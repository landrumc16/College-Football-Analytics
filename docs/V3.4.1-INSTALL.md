# Install College Football Analytics (Version 3.4.1)

Open https://landrumc16.github.io/College-Football-Analytics/ on your phone.

## Android (Chrome)
Open the browser menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. Confirm. Tap the College Football Analytics icon to launch.

## iPhone (Safari)
Open the Share menu and select **Add to Home Screen**; confirm the name and tap **Add**. Tap the icon on your home screen. Availability and appearance vary by browser and iOS version.

## Updates and offline use
New website releases become available the next time you open or refresh the app with an internet connection. You do not need to reinstall. This app requires an internet connection to load current football data; it deliberately does not serve cached scores or predictions as current.

## Troubleshooting
If the app appears unchanged after a release, close and reopen it while online, or refresh. If it cannot load, check your connection and try again. App-store installation is not part of this release.
