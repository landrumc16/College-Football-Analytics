# V5 historical evidence — 2018 final AP poll tie

**Season:** 2018. **Poll release:** January 8, 2019 (after the national championship).

## Independently corroborated finding

The final Associated Press football poll placed **Florida and Georgia jointly at No. 7** with **1,103 points each**. Texas was No. 9. Both schools must retain numeric rank 7, with no rank 8 assigned.

Evidence:
- College Poll Archive, 2018 final AP poll: https://collegepollarchive.com/football/ap/seasons.cfm?appollid=1151
- University of Florida athletics, retrospective acknowledgment of the tie: https://floridagators.com/news/2020/1/14/football-gators-finish-sixth-in-ap-top-25
- College Poll Archive, Georgia team-level final poll history: https://www.collegepollarchive.com/football/ap/appearances-final-team.cfm?teamid=73
- Stassen's final AP poll archive, explicitly denoting both as 7t: https://www.stassen.com/preseason/final-ap-poll/2018.html

**Source caveat:** Some university media-guide tables render the two 1,103-point entries as sequential 7 and 8 even though the historical poll archive and Florida's athletics department explicitly recognize a tie. Do not derive official rank from table row order.

## Publication and implementation rules

- Retain tied ranks as reported, **not** sequential row indices.
- Use AP poll ranks and points only for AP-derived features; do not substitute Coaches rankings.
- Hype calculations must not reward or penalize Florida versus Georgia because of an invented rank 8.
- Before marking the 2018 poll source independently verified in the production data contract, check the **actual retained canonical poll rows**, identity mappings, source dates and checksums against this evidence. This document verifies the external tie; it does **not** attest to uninspected internal poll files.
- Do not infer that all 2016–2025 AP or Coaches poll observations are verified from this one cross-source check.
