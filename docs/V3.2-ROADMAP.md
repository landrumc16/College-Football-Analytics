# College Football Analytics — V3.2 Model Accuracy Roadmap

Status: **development started** on `v3.2-development`. Production `main` remains the deployed V3.1 baseline.

## Objective
Improve out-of-sample prediction quality and explain the differences between our rankings and public polls. Never promote a model solely on in-sample fit or retrospective reconstruction.

## Milestones
1. **Audit and baseline.** Inventory pregame coverage, accuracy, Brier score, log loss, calibration, margin MAE, FBS–FBS vs FBS–FCS coverage, and weekly/seasonal breakdowns. Separate live archived predictions from retrospective walk-forward evaluations. Publish reproducible diagnostics.
2. **Candidate experiments.** Test opponent quality, strength of schedule, home-field effects, preseason-prior decay, FCS treatment, and probability calibration independently. Keep frozen workbook priors, team roster, and incumbent prediction formula unchanged in production.
3. **Leakage-free historical evaluation.** Use chronological pre-week predictions, holdout seasons, identical eligible games and FCS policies, uncertainty intervals, and paired comparisons to incumbent. Do not fit and score on the same holdout.
4. **Poll comparison.** Add sourced AP, Coaches and CFP snapshots with poll date/week and coverage. Show model-vs-poll movement and missing polls explicitly; never invent unavailable polls or treat them as ground truth.
5. **Dashboard.** Expose model version, sample size, baseline/candidate metrics, calibration and poll comparisons with clear provenance and mobile smoke coverage.
6. **Release gate.** Run Python tests, historical backtests, frontend build, mobile/browser smoke and GitHub Pages live checks. Merge only if candidate improvement is robust and no regression; otherwise ship diagnostics without replacing incumbent.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Do not modify the frozen `workbook_2026_baseline` or 138-team roster.
- Do not retroactively label postgame forecasts as pregame.
- Do not discard FCS results: retain them in modeling and report FBS-only evaluation separately.
- No future-game, later-week, or post-kickoff information in evaluated forecasts.
- Do not let a missing poll or a small sample produce a misleading claim of model superiority.
- Keep changes in large, testable increments and avoid speculative architecture work.

## First implementation chunk
Create a deterministic diagnostic scorecard from historical backtest observations, including weekly coverage and paired model-vs-baseline metrics. Add edge-case tests for empty groups, FCS, ties, and missing data. Do not alter live prediction calculations.

## Definition of done
A reproducible model evaluation and clear dashboard evidence, passing CI and published production smoke checks. A new model is optional and requires statistically and practically credible holdout improvement.
