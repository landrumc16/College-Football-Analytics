## Decision

**Retain incumbent** for V3.2. The ridge-strength-7 candidate is a research lead only; its tiny same-sample improvement is not an independent holdout validation. No live formula or frozen workbook changes are authorized by this analysis. The separate 2022–2025 V3 historical Elo study is a different model family and must not be represented as validation of these 2026 workbook candidates.

## Release safeguards

Keep FBS–FCS results visible but distinct from FBS–FBS metrics; retain the historical replay disclaimer; verify the production build contains the new diagnostics and poll comparisons are sourced. CI success is necessary but does not replace deployment and live-site verification.
